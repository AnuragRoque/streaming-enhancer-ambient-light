What it does

One extension, a better picture everywhere

It sits quietly on the video and shows a small toolbar when you move the mouse. Everything is also in the popup.

Quality lock

When your connection dips, streaming players drop to 480p or 720p. QualityLock takes the low options away, so the player waits a moment instead of going blurry.

Ambient light & blur fill

The black bars glow with the colours at the picture’s edges, or show a soft blurred copy of the scene, like YouTube’s ambient mode.

RGB light

Turn the bars into a virtual LED strip: Wave, Cycle, Breathe or Static, in your colours and at your speed.

Works on Netflix & Prime too

Your own backdrop

Put any picture behind the video, from a cinema curtain to a favourite wallpaper. The bars show its edges.

Fill, zoom & aspect

Remove black bars, stretch or zoom from 25 to 300%, pick 4:3 to 32:9 or any custom ratio, rotate and mirror.

Picture tuning

Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, warmth and sharpness, with presets like Cinema and Night. Hold to compare.

Speed & timeline

0.25× to 4× speed that sticks between videos, a timeline with hover previews, A-B loop and snapshots.

Mouse controls

Hold right-click and scroll for volume. Pick any mouse button, like the side buttons, to skip back or forward.

Viewing tools

Picture-in-Picture (even where sites switch it off), fill the browser window, lights off, and stats for nerds.

Where it works

Made for streaming, works on any video

The toolbar and effects work on any HTML5 video. The quality lock works on the big streaming services and on sites that use standard streaming formats.

Quality lock

  • Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, each with its own setting
  • Other sites that stream in DASH or HLS
  • Works within what the service already sends you. It never unlocks a resolution your plan or browser doesn’t get.

Toolbar & effects

  • Any HTML5 video, including YouTube and players embedded in other pages
  • RGB light, Image, zoom, picture, speed work everywhere, protected streams included
  • Ambient light, blur fill, snapshots: sites without DRM, like YouTube. Browsers don’t let extensions read protected video.
  • Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Firefox isn’t supported yet.

Nothing leaves your device

No account, no analytics, no ads and no servers of ours. Your settings only sync through your own browser account.

Install from GitHub

  1. On GitHub, choose Code → Download ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Play a video and press Alt+Z to show the toolbar.

FAQ

Questions

Will it give me 4K if my plan doesn’t include it?

No. It only stops the player dropping below the best quality the service already sends you. Your plan, your browser and the service’s own quality setting still set the ceiling.

Why don’t ambient light and blur fill work on Netflix?

Netflix, Prime Video and most paid services protect their video with DRM, and browsers never let extensions read those frames. RGB light and the Image backdrop don’t need the frames, so they work there.

Is it free? Does it collect my data?

It’s free, and it collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics or ads, and it doesn’t talk to any server of ours. The privacy policy has the details.

A site looks broken. What do I do?

Open the QualityLock popup, switch off On this site on the Now tab and reload the page. QualityLock then stays completely out of that site. Please report it on GitHub so it can be fixed.

Which keyboard shortcuts are there?

Alt+Z shows or hides the toolbar, Alt+X cycles fill and aspect presets, and Alt+. / Alt+, change speed. You can set more, like seek, PiP and backdrop, at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Is this made by Netflix, Amazon or YouTube?

No. QualityLock is an independent project and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by any streaming service.
